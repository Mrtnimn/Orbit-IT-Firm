Orbit IT Solutions respects your privacy. This policy describes the information we collect when you contact us, place an order, or subscribe to updates, and how we use it to provide our services.

01

Introduction

Orbit IT Solutions is a Nairobi-based business technology provider. We collect only information reasonably needed to respond to enquiries, arrange services, verify orders, and communicate with customers.

We do not ask for information that is unrelated to delivering or discussing Orbit's services.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive the following information:

Data You Provide

  • Full name and contact details
  • Business and service requirements
  • Order and payment reference details
  • Messages and communication preferences

Automated Data

  • Basic browser and device information
  • Pages visited and technical logs
  • Approximate location when provided or permitted
  • Information needed to keep the site secure
03

How We Use Your Information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare and deliver services, verify orders, communicate important updates, and improve the website.

Respond to service enquiries
Coordinate orders and payment references
Send relevant service updates
Ensure security and verification
Improve clarity and website performance
Provide customer support
04

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We share information only when it is needed to provide a requested service, process an order, protect the website, or meet a legal obligation.

With Your Permission

We may share information with your permission when another provider needs it to support your project or when you ask us to contact someone on your behalf.

For Legal Compliance

We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to prevent fraud, investigate misuse, or protect people and property.

  • Comply with applicable regulations and legal requirements
  • Enforce our service agreements
  • Address security threats and fraudulent activity
  • Safeguard user rights and property
05

Data Security

Orbit uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please contact us promptly if you notice a concern.

Secure connections

Use HTTPS when the website is hosted in production.

Limited access

Information is shared with people who need it for the agreed work.

Careful handling

We avoid collecting information that is not needed for the service.

06

Your Rights and Choices

You may ask what information Orbit holds about you, request corrections, request deletion where appropriate, or withdraw permission for optional communications.

Access Review your personal data anytime
Correction Update inaccurate information
Deletion Request removal of your data
Restriction Limit how we process your information
07

Changes to This Policy

Orbit may update this policy when our services, technology, or legal responsibilities change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Material changes will be presented clearly on this page. Please review the policy before submitting new information.

08
