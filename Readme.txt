Orbit IT Solutions
===================

Orbit IT Solutions is a Nairobi-based technology service provider for small and medium-sized
businesses. The website presents office networking, IT support, and business web
development services, with an online e-commerce store platform that designed with service selection, cart review, M-Pesa payment guidance, WhatsApp order confirmation, and direct contact options for users.

Website pages
-------------
- index.html: services, cart, payment guidance, and the firm's contact information
- service-details.html: Orbit's delivery approach and service capabilitie
- privacy.html: privacy information for enquiries, orders, and subscriptions
- terms.html: website, service, order, and payment terms
- 404.html: recovery page for unavailable URLs

Deployment notes
----------------
The static pages can be hosted both locally or on remote deployment services.
